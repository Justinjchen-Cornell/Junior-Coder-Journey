## 📜 Our Agreement (the soul of this repo)

1. 🎈 **Accompaniment first, documentation second** — play 10 minutes, then write 15
2. 🚫 **No tests, no comparisons, no corrections** — if it goes wrong, "the robot misheard"
3. 🌱 **Missed weeks are fine; quitting is not** — no guilt, just restart this week
4. ⭐ **The only KPI is the happiness rating**
5. 🔑 **On Youyou's 18th birthday, everything here belongs to him** — including the right to delete
